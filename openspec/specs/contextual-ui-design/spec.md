# contextual-ui-design Specification

## Purpose

Produce reviewable visual designs grounded in the actual product through a maintained upstream workflow, preserving project identity, authorized effects and the limits of rendered evidence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: UI design starts from the actual product
TH SHALL offer `ui-design` for new UI, refinement and explicitly requested redesign. The current general agent SHALL establish the affected surface, users, primary task, content, constraints and requested endpoint from available product context and incumbent components, tokens, assets and supplied references. Refinement SHALL preserve established identity, stack, component conventions and status meanings unless their replacement is authorized. A material missing scope or identity decision SHALL be resolved before dependent work; TH SHALL NOT invent an example application or infer redesign authority from a refinement request.

#### Scenario: An operational screen is refined
- **WHEN** the operator requests improvement of an existing operational interface
- **THEN** the design prioritizes its actual work and content, reuses incumbent components and identity, and keeps changes within the requested surface

#### Scenario: A new or replacement identity is requested
- **WHEN** the authorized work creates new UI or explicitly replaces an existing visual identity
- **THEN** the workflow derives direction from the product's users, task and constraints and makes unresolved material choices visible before dependent implementation

### Requirement: Impeccable preparation is selected and owned
At `ui-design` entry or resumption, TH SHALL resolve the active runtime's official Impeccable installation, installed instructions, version or revision, selected scope and required capabilities. It SHALL distinguish installed, discoverable and usable states, reuse a healthy capability and perform authorized preparation or repair through the official owner route only for the selected need. Default preparation SHALL omit optional upstream hooks. TH SHALL inspect actual installer/update effects, preserve unrelated configuration and independently owned files, and refrain from preparing inactive runtimes or forcing an invalid ownership record. A maintenance operation affecting additional providers SHALL require authorization covering those effects or remain pending with a concrete limitation.

#### Scenario: The selected capability is healthy
- **WHEN** the active runtime can perform the requested visual work with an existing installation
- **THEN** TH reuses it without redundant installation or an automatic update and records its actual capability state

#### Scenario: Preparation would affect another provider
- **WHEN** the supported maintenance route affects providers beyond the selected runtime or cannot establish owned files safely
- **THEN** TH preserves the existing installation, uses any supported usable capability and reports the exact unresolved preparation scope without inventing a narrower flag or forcing replacement

### Requirement: Visual methods remain upstream
TH SHALL read and follow the installed Impeccable instructions and matching supported playbooks for the selected work through the active host's native capabilities. It SHALL pass the product context, objective, authorized effects and output constraints without vendoring or rewriting the upstream design, critique, color or tool procedures. Upstream advice SHALL NOT enlarge operator authorization or change native permissions and coordination. Source templates with unresolved placeholders SHALL NOT substitute for installed instructions. Supported fallback routes SHALL retain upstream semantics; unavailable rendering, launcher or service capabilities SHALL be reported without claiming equivalent execution.

#### Scenario: The launcher cannot run
- **WHEN** the installed workflow provides a supported fallback after a launcher refusal or failure
- **THEN** TH announces that limitation, reads the existing product/design context and follows the permitted playbook without claiming launcher success

#### Scenario: An external direction capability is unavailable
- **WHEN** the selected upstream method depends on an unavailable service or tool
- **THEN** TH performs supported independent work and reports the missing capability rather than inventing an offline equivalent

### Requirement: Unsettled visual direction uses contextual references
When identity or color direction is unsettled or its replacement is authorized, TH SHALL perform bounded internet research after reading incumbent assets and supplied references. Selected sources SHALL relate to the product's audience, content, use scene or intended identity; TH SHALL retain source links, relevant observations and adaptation reasons instead of copying another identity or selecting a generic trending palette. Alternatives SHALL be compared only when they resolve a material open direction and SHALL use the same actual representative product screen. The selected upstream workflow SHALL map direction to semantic color roles and assess applicable accessibility, interaction states and themes while retaining established brand and status conventions within scope.

#### Scenario: Color direction remains open
- **WHEN** a product has no settled palette and a material choice remains
- **THEN** relevant references inform meaningfully different treatments on the same representative screen, with reasons for adapting the chosen direction and upstream semantic/accessibility evaluation

#### Scenario: Refinement retains an established palette
- **WHEN** the requested refinement preserves a settled brand and color direction
- **THEN** TH reuses it without requiring fresh internet research or a palette comparison

### Requirement: Visual design respects existing workflow continuity
TH SHALL reuse `workspace`, `design` and `sketch` under their existing contracts and preserve canonical OpenSpec intent when present. `design` SHALL remain the functional-first solution proposal, and the required frontend wireframe SHALL be presented before implementation of the affected surface; a high-fidelity visual result SHALL NOT silently replace that structural preview. A design-only request SHALL end at a reviewable visual result and unresolved decisions without production edits or implicit pipeline activation. An explicit request to implement or improve the application SHALL continue through the authorized scoped changes and relevant validation without another blanket approval; missing material decisions or new effects outside authorization SHALL remain explicit.

#### Scenario: Only a visual design is requested
- **WHEN** the operator requests UI design without implementation
- **THEN** TH presents the reviewable visual result in the selected workspace or explicit destination, states unresolved decisions and stops before production changes

#### Scenario: Implementation is already requested
- **WHEN** the operator explicitly requests improvement or implementation of an application surface
- **THEN** TH presents or reuses an accurate wireframe, reconciles material decisions with canonical intent and continues authorized implementation and validation without an added general approval ceremony

### Requirement: Project design authority and workspace evidence remain coherent
TH SHALL keep upstream-owned product/design records, configuration and surface/artifact state at their supported owner-project locations. The selected local or Obsidian workspace SHALL retain useful dependency identities, capability limits, reference research, adaptation rationale and links to visual decisions and verification evidence. TH SHALL reuse established project context and SHALL NOT create competing design authority or a second workspace merely because the runtime, checkout or workflow changes.

#### Scenario: A design is refined in a later session
- **WHEN** the effort resumes with existing upstream project context and a selected workspace
- **THEN** TH reads those current owner records and retained decision/evidence links, preserving a single direction and reporting stale or contradictory input before dependent work

### Requirement: Visible results and validation limits are explicit
TH SHALL preserve and report the reviewable visual output and applicable upstream finish evidence, including actual scope, relevant rendered screens, states, themes or device classes and any omissions. Explicit implementation SHALL receive scoped project checks and rendered assessment through the selected upstream workflow. Existing screenshots or fixtures SHALL be checked for freshness and SHALL NOT establish an unperformed fresh render. If the application or a required capability cannot run, TH SHALL retain supported partial results and label fresh rendered verification pending. Dependency readiness, source coverage, successful commands and structural repository tests SHALL NOT be presented as proof of visual quality or general improvement.

#### Scenario: Scoped implementation can be rendered
- **WHEN** the authorized interface changes are implemented and the application can run
- **THEN** TH runs relevant project checks, inspects the actual affected interface through the upstream workflow and retains the required visual evidence and remaining coverage limits

#### Scenario: The application cannot run
- **WHEN** an unavailable runtime prevents a fresh render
- **THEN** TH reports the cause, retains any supported reviewable partial artifact or sufficiently fresh fixture as qualified evidence, and leaves fresh rendered verification pending without claiming completion of that check
