## Approach

Choose the smallest readable-form correction after reproducing the failure. Treat malformed, obfuscated and unrelated commands under the current policy rather than adding another parser. Evaluator fixtures never execute the destructive input.

## Compatibility

General shell parsing, adversarial completeness, new guarded destinations, new hooks or broader secret detection. Existing native permissions and accepted direct-mode authority remain in effect.

## Validation

Use the scenarios in the delta and the existing relevant checks. Instruction-only changes receive behavioral inspection, not tests pinned to prose.
