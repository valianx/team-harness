After PR publication and authorized local installation, compare immutable baseline/candidate instructions in isolated A/B trials with identical model, effort, evidence and dispatch per runtime. Keep the reusable runner, fixtures, transcripts and metrics outside the product diff. Score material defects and controls, record native token/cache usage and time, and manually adjudicate findings. ## Risks and rollback

Extra context can increase consumption or cause false positives and unnecessary serialization. Scope reads to relevant changes, preserve independent test design and allow zero findings. Reverting the three canonical edits and regenerating restores prior guidance; retained measurement evidence does not become product policy.
