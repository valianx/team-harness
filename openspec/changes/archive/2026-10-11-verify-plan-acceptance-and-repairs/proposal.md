## Why

Plans can schedule acceptance before its producers, omit required scenarios from commands, or weaken agreed guarantees during repair. Issue #691 proposes focused guidance; its effectiveness remains to be measured.

## What Changes

- Explain real acceptance prerequisites and planned scenario coverage in shared spec/pipeline guidance.
- Review plan repairs with affected dependencies and preserved guarantees; group material findings without losing evidence and report uncertain origin honestly.
- Supply optional delta/history and the canonical plan-reviewer contract to the single explicitly requested native review assignment, including Codex's existing role mapping.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `four-phase-development`: acceptance prerequisites, command coverage and planning-only boundaries.
- `specialist-coordination-protocol`: bounded corrected-plan review with optional history and preserved guarantees.

## Impact

Three canonical sources and their generated distributions; existing native review dispatch; release metadata and the two living specifications. Evaluation artifacts remain in the task workspace.

## Non-Goals

No new roles, models, task fields, review rounds, approval gates or evaluation framework. No automatic plan-review invocation, product acceptance during planning, or claim of reduced cost or defects before comparative evidence.
