## Why

Agents expand documentation with repeated prose, fixed page sets and mandatory diagrams. The operator needs a TH guide used for writing and reviewing only necessary content.

## What Changes

- Ship write-documents as a concise editorial guide with upstream attribution.
- Route documentation, spec and specialist assignments to it.
- Make documenter pages and visuals depend on reader needs; align documentation QA.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `document-writing`: concise drafting and review with preserved facts and scope.

## Impact

Shared skills and agent instructions, generated runtime distributions and documentation QA criteria.

## Non-Goals

AI authorship detection, word-count quotas, new external dependencies or changes to document format tooling.
