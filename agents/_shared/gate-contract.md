# Decisions during a workflow

TH stage names describe progress. They do not add an execution or publication
permission layer.

The coordinator presents material choices with enough evidence to decide and
uses the operator's answer in its task context. Existing authorization persists.
Ask again only when a new decision or scope change needs it, not because a
stage, review label or historical record lacks a nonce.

Native permissions and the operator's request govern outward actions. Reviewer
findings inform judgment; they are not gate releases.
