# Compose and judge the architecture

The first view is a compact, useful overview of the complete architecture at its
initial fitted zoom. The agent owns composition decisions and visual corrections
before asking the operator to review the result.

## Preserve meaning while composing

Reuse the audience, source context and operator-approved preferences or examples
in the conversation and bound workspace. Keep their source and scope traceable;
only actual operator acceptance makes a candidate an approved reference. Keep
these preferences in the bound context rather than adding global configuration.
Resolve layout choices from this evidence; ask only for a missing material source
or audience decision.

Apply [reference-led flow composition](../../excalidraw-diagram/references/composition.md)
for functional zones, repeated components, relationship lanes and label placement.

Retain the full component, group and relationship inventory, identities and source
facts in the model and complete overview. Use concise, recognizable names and
relationship labels; keep full wording, explanations and evidence accessible in
details. Compact names must preserve distinctions between components and flows.

Use source-supported grouping to expose ownership, deployment, trust boundaries
or processing stages relevant to the audience. Preserve established boundaries;
empty space alone is not a reason to invent a group or change its membership.
Adapt graph backgrounds and component appearance to the context while preserving
the standard temporary controls and stable 90% canvas framing.

## Compare actual compositions

For automatic composition, compare a bounded set of arrangements using `layered`/`compact` results
and `auto` selection evidence, then supported model or shared token revisions.
Changing only color or the camera is not a new composition. Candidates retain the
same complete source inventory and boundaries.
An explicit mode computes that mode only. Retain candidate work/time and route
length/detour evidence alongside quality; unnecessary search is not improvement.
For declared flow intent, compare its satisfaction separately from geometry and text size; reject a folded spine or reversed support side even if the candidate is otherwise readable. The measured compact-flow alternative preserves this intent without operator coordinates; incompatible hierarchy remains a named limit.

Use [consumer inspection](layout-contract.md#inspect-and-repair) to capture the
actual artifact at 1440×900 and 1280×720. Confirm its model identity/revision and
fit the complete architecture; an explicitly navigated step can frame only part
of it. Compare screenshots and geometry
together, rejecting invalid geometry before choosing among readable candidates.
Prefer a useful canvas aspect over a tall narrow chain or wide shallow strip with
mostly empty canvas.
Inspect the visible names and relationship labels at that fitted scale, hierarchy,
group whitespace, route detours, crossings and the path the audience must follow.
Diagram font size multiplied by fit zoom estimates on-screen logical text size;
pixel density changes sharpness, not that reading size. Read the output rather
than accepting a fit ratio, small bounds or passing collision checks as proof.

Select the candidate that best explains the whole architecture with readable
labels and clear groups. Favor shorter unambiguous wording and a better arrangement
before reducing shared spacing or typography; preserve measured clearances.
Do not hide relationships, merge distinct components or falsify topology to fit.

## Correct before presenting

Use the initial composition and at most two corrective revisions. After each
revision, inspect the actual fitted result and renew affected source, geometry and
rendered checks. This agent correction budget is separate from the engine's
maximum three measurement/layout passes for each model generation.

When the overview is weak, change the supported arrangement, concise labels or
source-supported grouping that caused it, then compare again. Perspectives can
explain dense flows, ownership or dependencies; they supplement the complete
overview and retain relationship membership and continuation context.

Keep rejected candidates, screenshots and concise comparison reasons in the
operational workspace. Deliver the selected artifact with its visual evidence;
record a preference only after the operator actually accepts it.

If the correction budget ends without a useful result, retain the full model and
state the concrete remaining limit: affected labels, groups or flows, viewport and
observed failure. Identify the smallest supported next change. Do not claim that
arbitrary dense architectures fit readably or present the unresolved result as ready.
