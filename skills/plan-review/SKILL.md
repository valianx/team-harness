---
name: plan-review
description: Critique a proposed plan or OpenSpec change with independent judgment.
---

Resolve the plan named by $ARGUMENTS or the active task. Read its objective,
relevant code, OpenSpec intent, acceptance scenarios and material decisions.

Use an available plan reviewer for an independent critique. Ask for concrete
defects, omissions, alternatives and supporting source locations. The review
does not edit the plan; the coordinator evaluates its recommendations with the
operator's wider context and applies worthwhile corrections.

Report useful findings, coverage and limits. Follow up on material uncertainty
when it affects the decision. A review is not a pipeline release or a substitute
for specialist evidence it did not actually collect.
