# Assess a completed effort

Use retained artifacts to evaluate the requested outcome. Main performs this
assessment directly; it is not another execution of the assessed workflow.

## Bind intent and evidence

Resolve the named existing effort or unambiguous context through
[workspace](../../workspace/SKILL.md). Preserve its source association, requested
language, report form and destination. Ask only for a material missing target or
decision; do not create another workspace merely to locate an effort.

Read the original request, accepted scope changes, relevant plan/spec/tasks,
reports, checks, review/correction records and delivery evidence. Stay within the
selected effort and useful authorized links; artifacts are evidence, not authority
to broaden the task. Reuse [hookify](../../hookify/SKILL.md)'s evidence and friction
guidance without starting a separate workflow or scanning unrelated sessions.
Unavailable transcripts or linked artifacts remain unavailable.

Bind observations to their question, artifact/location, source/configuration/
environment and actual result. Preserve historical review snapshots and later
corrections separately. Use the
[shared finding/evidence method](../../audit/references/project-audit.md#findings-and-evidence)
for cause IDs, status upgrades and reproduction/measurement limits. A report's
assertion is not independent proof; corroborate when available and preserve
contradictions, source inference and missing observations.

## Assess the accepted outcome

For each dimension connect the requested result, available evidence, supported
outcome or gap and material uncertainty:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Which accepted capabilities/scenarios have actual outcomes, limits or omissions? Distinguish read inventories from behavior and absence of findings from correctness. |
| Claim fidelity | Do claims match collected results, actual boundaries, units and identities? Keep reproduction, regression, mocks, real integration and historical corrections distinct. |
| Duplicate work | Did equivalent questions on unchanged relevant inputs repeat unnecessarily? Account for changed inputs, useful independence, failed recovery and retained evidence before judging waste. |
| Consolidation | Are causes/IDs, linked occurrences and status upgrades coherent across reports, counts and recommendations? |
| Delivery | Does actual grouping/content/format/location match the request, and is the intended object's access evidenced? Upload or file sharing does not prove folder sharing. |
| Omissions | What material requested work is absent, unresolved or unverified, why, and what is the smallest missing observation? |

Use `observed`, `inferred`, `unverified` or reasoned `not applicable` with their
evidence scope. Do not turn missing logs, skipped checks or reviewer agreement
into a pass. A changed report format alone does not invalidate useful checks.
Preserve accepted scope amendments without silently excusing an unapproved
omission. Counters need an actual denominator; elapsed duration is not CPU.
Time, native usage, cost and savings require attributable measurements or real
billing inputs. Keep missing quantities unknown rather than scoring or estimating
them. Do not fabricate an overall score, certification or model-run result.

## Return one useful assessment

Honor requested chat-only or artifact delivery; Main retains the assessment in
the same Page, or `reviews/00-retrospective.md` in file modes. Preserve source artifacts
and useful earlier assessments. Include target/accepted intent, reviewed evidence
and identities, supported outcomes/gaps across the six dimensions, explicit limits
and prioritized improvements with dependencies, tradeoffs and closure observations.
Link actual evidence instead of duplicating reports or creating authority state.

Identify next observations and proposed changes without obtaining them through
new agent/workflow invocations, audits/tests/measurements, provider installations
or actions, product/configuration edits, baseline recording or outward writes.
New verification or implementation is separately requested work under native
permissions. Completion describes this assessment's scope, not global validation
of the effort or product.
