# Workflow status

Use [workspace](../workspace/SKILL.md) read-only to discover the configured local
or Obsidian efforts. Read each existing plan, tasks or handoff. Historical
`00-state.md` or control logs may supply additional context; do not impose v4
fields on v5 or require either schema for current work.

Report effort, workspace, observed progress, last known update and next unresolved
work. Unknown fields stay unknown. An old timestamp or absent tmux process alone
does not prove a run failed. No writes, administrative closure or repairs occur.

Use [the OpenSpec lifecycle](../spec/references/lifecycle.md) to list pending
archive or retirement in current/bound repositories, including direct work.
Checked boxes alone do not prove acceptance or completion. If optional trace
records exist, summarize them as observations rather than authority.
